## Welcome

Hello! Welcome to my personal blog. This is a tech blog built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/), where I'll share my programming experiences, technical notes, and project insights.

## Why Hugo?

- **Fast**: Hugo is written in Go, with blazing-fast build times
- **Simple**: Write in Markdown, focus on content
- **Flexible**: Rich themes and template system
- **Free deployment**: Completely free with GitHub Pages or Vercel
